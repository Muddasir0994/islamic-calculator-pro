# 🕌 Al-Mirath & Al-Zakat Pro (المیراث و الزکوٰۃ پرو)

An advanced, Shariah-compliant **Islamic Inheritance (Mirath), Zakat, Ushr & Munasakha Calculator** based on **Fiqh-e-Hanafi**, featuring exact fractional mathematics, automatic estate deductions, explainable Shariah exclusions (*Hajb*), Unborn Child (*Haml*) dual-scenario reserve calculation, and multi-unit property conversion.

## ✨ Advanced Features Over Standard Calculators

1. **Automatic Shariah Estate Deductions**:
   - Calculates Net Distributable Estate automatically after deducting Funeral expenses (`تجہیز و تکفین`), Debts & Unpaid Mahr (`قرض و حق مہر`), and Bequests (`وصیت`) with automatic `1/3` (`33.33%`) Shariah cap enforcement.
2. **Complete 29-Heir Hanafi Fara'id Engine**:
   - Covers all 6 categories: Spouses (`زوجین`), Descendants (`اولاد`), Ascendants (`اصول`), Siblings (`بھائی بہن`), Other Agnates (`دیگر عصبات`), and Distant Kindred (`ذوی الارحام`).
   - Handles **'Awl (`عول`)**, **Radd (`رد`)**, **Umariyyatan (`عمریتین`)**, **Asabah ma'al Ghair**, and **Tashih (`تصحیح`)** with exact integer fractions (`سہام`).
3. **Explainable Fiqhi Proof (`شرعی دلیل اور وجہِ محرومی`)**:
   - Explains why each heir received their share or why specific relatives were excluded (*Mahjoob*).
4. **Haml (`حمل کی وراثت` — Unborn Child Dual-Scenario Reserve)**:
   - Computes both Male & Female baby scenarios and shows the safe immediate payout plus reserved share (`موقوف حصہ`).
5. **Munasakha (`مناسخہ` — Multi-Stage Inheritance)**:
   - Seamless Stage-2 inheritance distribution when an heir passes away before estate distribution.
6. **Multi-Unit Land Converter & Advanced Zakat + Agricultural Ushr**:
   - Distributes property in **Acres, Kanal, Marla, Sq. Yards, and Sq. Feet**.
   - Calculates Zakat across Gold (24K/22K/21K/18K), Silver (Tola/Gram/Ounce), Cash, Committee/BC, Provident Fund, Trade Stock, and Agricultural Ushr (`10%` / `5%`).
